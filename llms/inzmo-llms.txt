# INZMO

> INZMO is a European insurtech (founded 2017) delivering 100% digital, embedded insurance for consumer goods and rental deposits. Products cover mobile phones, laptops/tablets, bicycles, e-scooters, and rental deposit warranties, sold direct-to-consumer and embedded into partner checkout flows. INZMO offers partners an embedded-insurance API for real-time policy issuance, payment, and delivery of policy documents, and applies AI/LLMs to fraud prevention and claims handling. Portfolio company of 500 Global.
